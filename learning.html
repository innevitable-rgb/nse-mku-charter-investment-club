Investment fundamentals

Investment fundamentals introduces members to the principles of personal finance and investing.
The package explains how money grows and over time, the importance of saving, budgeting and setting financial goals before making investment decisions.

Members will also gain an understanding of common investment opportunities available in kenya, including money market funds, treasury bills and bonds, reits, shares abd other lonf term wealth building options.
The goal is to equip members wuth practical knowledge to make informaed financial decisions with confidence.
